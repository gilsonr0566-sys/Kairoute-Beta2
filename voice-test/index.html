Kairoute voice test

Free voices that run inside the app, on your phone or tablet. No account, no fees, nothing sent to a server. Turn your volume up, then tap a voice and Play.

1. Kokoro voices (best quality)

The first Play downloads the voice engine once (about 92 MB, Wi-Fi recommended). After that it is kept on the device and starts in a few seconds.

2. Your phone's own voice (for comparison)

Built into Android and iPhone. Nothing to download, but it usually sounds more robotic.

3. Other languages (Piper voices)

Kokoro in the browser speaks English only (American and British). For other languages we test Piper, a second free engine. Each language is its own one-time download of about 60 MB.
